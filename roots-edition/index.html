Parent's Weekly Reset

Daily Checkpoints (1–3)

  • Iron 7 mg — iron-rich food daily; pair plant iron with vitamin C.
  • Calcium 700 mg — reliable anchors.
  • Vitamin D 15 mcg / 600 IU.
  • B12 0.9 mcg — fish, eggs, or a reliable fortified source.
  • Iodine 90 mcg — use iodized salt appropriately, plus seafood/fortified foods as relevant; do not increase toddler salt just to chase iodine.
  • Protein 13 g · Zinc 3 mg (rises to 5 mg by ages 4–8) · Choline 200 mg.
  • Fiber 19 g (rises to 25 g by ages 4–8) — pair with plenty of fluids.
  • Sodium ceiling — keep well under 1,200 mg/day (ages 1–3); a ceiling, not a target.
